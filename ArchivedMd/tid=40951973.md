```bash
export cert_hash_old=$(openssl x509 -inform PEM -subject_hash_old -in charles-ssl-proxying-certificate.pem | head -1)
mv charles-ssl-proxying-certificate.pem $cert_hash_old.0
```
当然理论上嫌麻烦也可以直接把客户端解包之后塞个 network-security-config 再重打包，前提是没做签名校验。
